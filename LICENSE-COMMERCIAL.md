## 🏢 When Do You Need a Commercial License?

A separate commercial license is required if you intend to use OpenJevPro for commercial purposes, including but not limited to:

1. **Enterprise Production Deployment**: Running OpenJevPro within internal business workflows, corporate infrastructure, or production pipelines.
2. **Product Integration**: Embedding or bundling OpenJevPro into proprietary or commercial software, platforms, or hardware.
3. **Hosted / Cloud Services**: Offering OpenJevPro as a hosted service, managed API, or white-label solution to third parties for a fee.

To obtain a commercial license, apply for enterprise support, or request custom SLA terms:
